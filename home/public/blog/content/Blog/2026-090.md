This is my [September 2026 #Emacs Carnival](https://www.emacswiki.org/emacs/Carnival) entry.
Back in the mists of time, there was [Zippy the Pinhead](https://zippythepinhead.com/).  For a while, zippy quotes were included as part of emacs, but they were not free, so they were removed.

Here, to help you "understand" Zippy are a couple of the comic strips [Zippy One](https://zippythepinhead.com/Merchant2/merchant.mv?Screen=PROD&Store_Code=ZTP&Product_Code=30-Mar-02&Category_Code=) [Zippy Two](https://zippythepinhead.com/Merchant2/merchant.mv?Screen=PROD&Store_Code=ZTP&Product_Code=16-Feb-02&Category_Code=), but "understand" is the exactly the wrong word when it comes to Zippy.

Now, add to that even less context.  Just the text of random zippy quotes and you've got ... well, you tell me what you've got?

The last thing that happens in my emacs startup config displays a random Zippy quote on the status line.  Here's the code.

<!--more-->


## <span class="section-num">1</span> Zippy haunting my emacs config {#zippy-haunting-my-emacs-config}

```text
* All's Well That Ends Well, YOW !!!
   Because, well, you've got to be able to M-x psychoanalyze-pinhead
   to fill a buffer and pass the time....

   Discussion of the demise of zippy in Emacs here:
    - http://forums.fedoraforum.org/showthread.php?t=302313

   You'll need a copy of the zippy quotes.  I pulled from here LONG ago..
   https://github.com/shentonfreude/dot-emacs/blob/master/yow.lines

#+BEGIN_SRC emacs-lisp :exports code
  (message "init.el finished. Yow !")
  (setq yow-file "~/.emacs.d/etc/yow.lines")
  (global-set-key "\ey" 'yow)		; my ANCIENT keybinding

  ;; This function gives me a zippy quote on the status line after
  ;; everything else in the config has settled down.
  ;; I'm sure there are better/cleaner ways...
  (defun display-yow-in-status-line ()
    "Display the result of yow in the status line once after 1 second of idle time."
    (interactive)
    (run-with-idle-timer 1 nil
                         (lambda ()
                           (let ((yow-output (yow)))
                             (message "%s" yow-output)))))
  (display-yow-in-status-line)

  #+END_SRC
```


## <span class="section-num">2</span> psychoanalyze-pinhead {#psychoanalyze-pinhead}

There also used to be "M-x psychoanalyze-pinhead" where you could endlessly fill a buffer with Rogerian analysis of zippy quotes: <https://www.elsewhere.org/journal/archives/2001/10/07/psychoanalyze-pinhead/> I mean, who needs LLMs when you can watch a live analysis session of Zippy. There are some issues that keep the original code  from  working in modern Emacsen, and I meant to fixed them in time for this Emacs Carnival post, but didn't quite get there.

There was also "M-x psychoanalyze-flammer" which analyzed a stream of inflammatory insults.

## <span class="section-num">1</span> Emacs Carnival, December 2025 - "The People of Emacs" {#emacs-carnival-december-2025-the-people-of-emacs}

This is a writing prompt for [Emacs Carnival](https://www.emacswiki.org/emacs/Carnival) for December 2025.

The basic ask is to write about "Emacs people you've known." Preference is given to people who influenced you, who inspired you, who taught you, or who bent your mind. Bonus points if they are people you've known in "real life" (offline). Of course, stories, character sketches, [tributes](http://curious.galthub.com/blog/steve/) and [poetry](http://curious.galthub.com/blog/2023-12-09/) are encouraged.

Emacs is the most people-centric technology I've ever used.  I'm willing to bet that's true for others as well.

Who are your "People of Emacs"?
